## 4. Assessment Methodology & Mode Mapping
- **Mode A: Immediate Knowledge Check (`KnowledgeCheck`)**
  - Directly positioned after the theoretical exposition and mathematical formula callouts in every lesson.
  - Instant feedback on click: immediate positive reinforcement for correct answers with the underlying academic condition; constructive contextual hint and retry option for incorrect answers.
- **Mode B: Section Assessment (`SectionAssessment`)**
  - Positioned at the conclusion of each lesson module.
  - Multi-question evaluation set with free navigation and answer revision prior to submission.
  - Single prominent action: `Submit Answers & Check Results` / `تسليم الإجابات وتصحيح الاختبار`.
  - Post-submission: score summary percentage, mastery badge, individual question breakdown, contextual hints for wrong answers, and full bilingual academic explanations.
- **Spaced Repetition Tracking (`SpacedReviewScheduler`)**
  - Performance from both Mode A and Mode B automatically calculates the next review date in `localStorage`: 1 day (1st correct), 3 days (2nd consecutive), 7 days (3rd consecutive), 14 days (4th consecutive), 30 days (5th consecutive).
  - Mistake resets interval to 1 day and flags topic as `Needs Review` in the **Topics to Review** queue.
