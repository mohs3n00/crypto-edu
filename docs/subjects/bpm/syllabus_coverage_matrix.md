## 2. Granular Syllabus Structure (4 Units, 15 Lessons)

### Unit 1: Foundations of Business Process Management & Modeling (مفاهيم وأسس إدارة ونمذجة عمليات الأعمال)
- **Lesson 1.1:** Course Overview, Historical Evolution & Perspectives (Adam Smith, Rummler-Brache, Business vs CS)
- **Lesson 1.2:** Digital Transformation Case Study: Canon HR Digitization (Challenge, Therefore™ Case Manager, Results)
- **Lesson 1.3:** Conceptual Foundations & The Essence of Modeling (Herbert Stachowiak's 3 Criteria, Weske & ABPMP Definitions)
- **Lesson 1.4:** Process Models, Instances & Enterprise Landscape (Weske Lifecycle, Process Hierarchy Levels, BPM Technology Stack)

### Unit 2: BPM Architecture, Lifecycle & Organizational Governance (معمارية دورة حياة العمليات والحوكمة التنظيمية)
- **Lesson 2.1:** Core Concepts & Organizational Commitment (Process vs Project, Functions vs End-to-End Processes, BPMS Suites)
- **Lesson 2.2:** Business Process Anatomy & Lifecycle Models (Input-Process-Output, 6-Phase Circular & Spiral Lifecycle, Maturity Factors)
- **Lesson 2.3:** Process Classifications & End-to-End Case Analysis (Continuous vs Ad Hoc, Simple vs Complex, IT Support Request Workflow)
- **Lesson 2.4:** Process Ownership & Modeling Standards (Process Owner Role, Purpose of Modeling, BPMN vs EPC vs UML Spectrum)

### Unit 3: BPMN 2.0 Syntax, Semantics & Collaborative Modeling (معيار نمذجة العمليات BPMN 2.0 والنمذجة التعاونية)
- **Lesson 3.1:** BPMN 2.0 Structural Taxonomy & Flow Objects (Events, Catching vs Throwing, Activities, Sub-processes, Transactions)
- **Lesson 3.2:** Gateways, Branching Semantics & Boundary Events (Exclusive XOR, Parallel AND, Inclusive OR, 1-Day Leave Timeout)
- **Lesson 3.3:** Swimlanes & Collaborative Orchestration (Pools vs Lanes, Message Flows, Customer-Vendor Lunch/Pizza Collaboration)
- **Lesson 3.4:** Practicing BPMN & Process Choreography (Weske Reseller-Buyer Fig 1.1-1.4, Process Variations, Edraw & Signavio Tools)

### Unit 4: Workflow Management, Execution Architectures & Management Tools (إدارة تدفقات العمل ومعمارية التنفيذ وأدوات الإدارة)
- **Lesson 4.1:** Workflow Management Fundamentals & Design Methodology (Automation, 4-Step Design Method, WfMS Capabilities, KPIs)
- **Lesson 4.2:** Execution Architectures & The WfMC Reference Model (Enactment Service, Engine vs WAPI, 5 Interfaces, Instance Lifecycle, IPO vs Constraints)
- **Lesson 4.3:** Workflow Control-Flow Patterns & Advanced Routing (AND/XOR/OR Splits/Joins, N-out-of-M Join, Discriminator, Invoice & Travel Cases)
- **Lesson 4.4:** Strategic Business Management Tools & Frameworks (Tools vs Methods, SWOT, Balanced Scorecard, OKRs, Lean vs Six Sigma, Porter's Five Forces, Scrum, PDCA)
